Robotics / Controls

Gait-based Control of Modular Snake Robots

Compliant, gait-based controllers I implemented on modular snake robots in CMU's Biorobotics Lab so they can cross unknown terrain.

The question How do we coordinate the motion of hyper-redundant snake robots while remaining computationally inexpensive?

Lab
CMU Biorobotics Lab
Advisor
Prof. Howie Choset

Background

Snake robots are highly articulated robots that can coordinate their many degrees of freedom to access areas which are otherwise unreachable by machinery. They possess an infinite number of configurations allowing them to conform to and locomote over irregular terrain.

Read more on the CMU Biorobotics Lab research page.
