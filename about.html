About

My Master’s in Mechanical Engineering is at Carnegie Mellon University (2023 – 2025), with a focus on robotics and adaptive control. In the Biorobotics Lab, under Prof. Howie Choset, I’ve implemented these algorithms on snake robots so they can traverse unknown terrain.

I earned my Bachelor’s in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras. There I contributed to Raftar Formula Racing, modeling, designing and manufacturing the suspension and brakes for our institution’s first electric racecar, the RFR23. In my senior year, I led the team to a podium finish at Formula Bharat 2023.
